\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This report aims to study the application of Machine Learning in Multi–class Cardiovascular Disease Prediction based on 12–lead ECG.
We shall revisit a prior work on this application in Part 1, critise their work is of poor quality and from evidence collected within this report, we claim that the results are not reproducible.
Therefore, in Part 2, we propose our solution to the same dataset, and observe more promising results with and without the use of DL feature extractors.
Empirically, we claim that, tree–based models perform relatively well on high–dimensional data, at an expense of computational complexity.
